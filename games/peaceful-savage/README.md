# Peaceful Savage: Light the Tree

A story game in a real-time 3D world, from the picture book _A Spark in the Dark_. One night, the Savage family's futuristic home has
gone grey, and the ancestor tree in the hall has nearly gone out: its trunk is three strands, and the third has lost its balance (it
bows out of the braid and its light pulses and fades). The player chooses who leads: **Kai** (11, the builder), **Zora** (8, the
star-gazer), **Mama Heerah** (whose markings light up to protect) or **Dr. Ali Savage** (Daddy). The others are found where the run
begins for them: Daddy in the fog of his home office, Kai on the porch building something, Zora in the meadow, Mama at the tree. Every
room greys or colours by itself as the story goes, and when the tree is lit the whole world takes its colour back.

Nobody is hurt in it. The sly snakes are ancestors with feelings nobody listened to; their whispers are tricks, and a true answer
heals them. The giant sly snake on the hill is all of them together, and the family heals it together. The game never says it heals or
treats the player: it says what it is.

## How a run goes

One level (the house, the meadow, the hill), with three subplots that interleave and a finale that needs all three:

1. **Sparky.** A hand on the tree wakes it and gives Savage Vision; the lamp that would not go out is helped with three hand-keys on the
   beat, and Sparky (the ancestors' whisper made visible) grins out of the light and follows the lead.
2. **The maridium threads.** Savage Vision shows the sly snakes, the clues and the threads. Each snake's whisper is answered truly (mirror
   force, from a breath and a look in a mirror, turns a believed trick back once), and it turns gold and drops its thread.
3. **The jackets and the hill.** With the six threads and Mama, the jackets are woven at her loom; with the family together and the
   huddle mastered, the hill calls. The giant sly snake rises; the family asks it in, huddles, breathes, plays the six hand-keys and names
   what it feels; it turns gold from the tail up and lets the ancestors in it go.

**The farewell** always happens at home, at the tree, wherever the snake was healed. The family stands before the tree as its third
strand lights fully; they turn, one after another, to face the screen as the ancestors fade in round the tree behind them, and hold
the big hug: the children in front, Daddy's hand on Kai's shoulder from behind, Kai's arm behind his sister to her far shoulder with
Mama's hand laid over his, and Zora holding Sparky in the middle; no arm crosses a face. The ancestors rise into the tree and fade; the
family dances out of the front door to the drums and up onto a stage that fades in on the lawn: microphones, speakers, a truss of
coloured lamps sweeping the stage, and a band of ancestors at the drums, the keys and the strings. For sixty seconds they sing and dance
to the screen with the ancestors dancing all round; the player takes one of the family at a time (Q, or a tap on a touch screen, passes
to the next), walks them about the stage (the arrows or WASD, or a drag) and gives a big move, a spin and a jump (E or Space). Enter
skips to the card. Then the stage fades and a hover car, a long open pod with not a straight line on it, glides onto the lawn on three
rings of gold light. The family climbs in and rides it round the meadow for twenty-six seconds: the same hands drive it (the arrows or
a drag) and lift it high over the trees (E, Space or a tap); left alone it cruises a figure of eight. It brings them home, they step
out and hold hands in a line on the lawn as the jackets light up gold, and lift off one by one, each flying a way of their own.

**The house, and what is under the meadow.** The house is the book's: one wave of a roof in purple scales edged with gold, tall over
the east wing and dipping to the door. Its windows are real: ovals of clear glass in gold frames cut through the outer walls (the
kitchen's great scooped window, the office's three, a round one at the end of the hall, one in each bedroom), so the rooms are seen
from the grass and the meadow from the rooms. There are five ways in: the front door; the office's tall window, up its round steps; the
kitchen's side door, at the top of a raised stair that curves round the corner of the house; and a sliding window in each bedroom. Their
glass panes slide aside as someone comes near. The grass goes all round the house, to a hedge on every side. Inside nothing has a right
angle: walls roll round at their ends, corners sweep, every room has a vault and every doorway a tall oval arch, and the furniture is
all pebbles. The swing's seat is a curved cross-section of mahogany. The giant snake is not seen until the end: it moves under the
meadow, a ridge of turned earth running over it, a loop of its back rolling out of the grass now and then. The hill it rests under is
plain grass; when the family comes in their jackets the ground there breaks open, clods flying, and it rises whole for the first time.

Each of the family has a lesson, lived rather than told: Zora makes and mixes things and learns to love the land (she plants seeds in the
meadow and tends them into bloom); Kai can build anything and learns structure, mindfulness and looking after himself (his tower goes up
a step at a time, with a breath in the middle); Daddy leads and learns that it is safe to be soft, turning what was handed down in pain
into joy; Mama senses what is coming and learns to say what she needs, as the ancestors call the family together.

**What the family makes and keeps.** Once their lesson is learned, each of the family makes something the family keeps (and each is a
milestone, so a collectible): Kai builds a brilliant holographic structure on his workbench over the tower he built again (a helix of
light with turning rings and a crystal at the top), Zora mixes a masterpiece where she was playing in the meadow (a swirl of every colour
on a little easel), Mama sings her song where she stood by the tree (gold notes that rise and circle), and Daddy releases a relic from the
drawer he opened (a gold amulet with a teal heart that floats free over his desk). Each is a short moment of hand-keys and breath that its
maker plays beside the lead; each stays where it was made for the rest of the run.

**Shapes of light.** The family's moves draw thin holographic shapes in the air, as the cover draws gold rings round Daddy's palm: each
hand-key its own (the Cup a bowl of light, the Comb its teeth, the Knot a loop that never ends, the Knock rings spreading from the palm,
the Split two circles parting, the Let-Go a spiral unwinding), a held breath a shield of light round the body, the breath let go a field
of loops through it, the centring at a mirror a flower of circles on the floor, and a dance a turning star. Each is drawn line by line
with a bright pen at its end, and fades when the move is done.

**Sparky's tug.** When the story stalls (twenty seconds with nothing found, healed, woven, learned or lit, and no moment under way),
Sparky flies on ahead along a gold thread to where the story goes next and tugs, and the line over the screen says to follow him. It
reads the state only and changes nothing in the run.

**Sitting, swinging, jumping, running.** E sits on what can be sat on (the couch, the beds, the chairs, the bench) and acting again
stands up; the swing in the meadow is ridden, each act a push higher, with the family watching from beyond the tree. Space jumps (it is
only seen); Shift, or a drag further, runs. The looking glasses and the pond show the world in them (the one in the lead's room at a
time, so the house is drawn again for one glass, not five).

On the way: the family moments (sit with Kai at his cracked tower, split Zora's silly cube, breathe with Mama, sit with Daddy in the
fog and knock), Daddy's locked drawer, pancakes, the card deck, the lamps, clues and inventors' relics in every room, tiny plasma to
gather, the lead's spark (their own light, dimmed near an unhealed snake and by a believed trick, filled by a breath and the truth), and
practice, which raises each hand-key, the breath and the huddle from new to mastered and the lead's level at the tree. Believe too many
tricks with the spark low and the lead goes grey and is taken gently into a coil of grey figures, for now: the next run begins in colour.

**Share cards.** At each turning point (Sparky, each one found, the tower, the garden in bloom, the drawer, each of the family's
creations, the jackets, and three times
when the tree is lit: the big hug, the show, and the lift-off) the page keeps a picture of the moment with its words, as a square post or
a tall story.
The Cards button shows them. The platform's frame blocks downloads and the share sheet, so the panel says how to save a card by hand;
letting a game download or share is a decision for the platform's owners.

**The camera** knows the characters and the moment: behind the lead as they walk, their profile on a sharp turn, the front on a lean,
wide for the family; in close on the hands as each hand-key rises and lands; on the chest and face for the Savage Breath; pulled back to
see a whole dance; and on the hill it cuts, on the beat, between the hands and the snake. The farewell has shots of its own: the tree, a
family portrait for the hug, the dance out of the door, the whole stage from the hillside for the show, a close pass along the joined
hands as the jackets light, and the lift-off. It never goes under the hill or through a wall: it keeps over the ground beneath it, and
its line to the lead clear of the ground and of the walls.

## Controls

- **Keyboard.** Left and right choose who leads; Enter starts. Arrows or WASD walk (seen from the camera), and with Shift held, run;
  E acts on what the prompt names (or sits, or stands up), and held, it breathes in (let go after four counts, then six out); Space
  jumps, and in a moment it is the act key too; Q opens Savage Vision;
  1 to 8 practise (1 and 2 answer, when there is a choice); P pauses. The buttons under the screen do the same, and End run sends the
  run as it stands.
- **Touch.** A swipe chooses who leads and a tap starts. In a run, a finger dragged on the screen walks (dragged further, it runs), a
  tap acts, and a finger held still breathes in; in a moment, the finger down is the act key, on time for the beat. Four buttons in the
  corner jump (in a moment, act), open vision, practise the skill most worth practising, and show the share cards.

The family found walks close at the lead's sides, not in single file (on the lead's own path through a doorway), and the camera rises
and pulls back a little for each of them, so nobody stands between it and the lead.

## Layout of the source

- `src/rules/`: the rules, a pure, seeded, whole-number state machine (millimetres, sixteen headings written out, axis-aligned walls),
  the story's moments, the garden and the tower, what the family makes (`create.ts`), greying, growth (spark, light, levels, mastery)
  and the 40 milestones.
- `src/session.ts`: one run as the player plays it, and its record. `src/page/run.ts` turns the player's hands into the rules' inputs.
- `src/page/`: the parts of the page (who leads, the readouts and spark bar, the moment panel, the prompt and toasts, the buttons, the
  keys and the touch screen, the share cards and their panel, and the end of a run played out before the result).
- `src/script/`: every word the game says, once: the hero cards, the moments' titles, the choices, the clues, relics and toasts.
- `src/view/`: the 3D view (three.js on WebGL 2), which reads the rules' state and changes nothing: the stage and its light, the
  storybook toon look (painted bands, a gold rim, ink lines, a moonlit duotone for grey rooms), the sky, the camera that knows the
  characters (`camera.ts`, kept out of walls and the hill by `clearance.ts`), where the found walk beside the lead (`beside.ts`), the
  family's procedural bodies (skinned, about sixty joints; sculpted human heads with a skull, cheekbones, a jaw, a chin, a nose and lips,
  their features painted on and real eyes in their sockets; coily hair on a full scalp and Daddy's freeform locs; and a two-bone reach for a hand
  laid exactly, as on a shoulder in the hug), the farewell (`farewell.ts`, `family-farewell.ts`) and its show (`show.ts`), the world
  (`world/`: the house, the meadow, the garden, the tree and its three strands, the giant sly snake, the ancestors who come home, the
  stage, the band's instruments and the dancing crowd, Sparky, the looking glasses and the pond, the swing, what the family makes) and
  the gold line-work of the magic, with its holographic shapes (`world/holograms.ts`, `world/sigils.ts`).
- `src/screen/`: the flat map for a device with no WebGL 2, and the card after a run. `src/guide.ts` is the first-screen card.
- `src/audio.ts`, `src/concert.ts`, `src/feedback.ts`: a lullaby, the show's groove and the effects, as data, and which effect each kind
  of news plays.
- `src/replay.ts`, `src/sample-run.ts`: the verifier the platform replays; the manifest pins its digest.
- `src/main.ts`: joins the above to the page and the platform.

## How runs are checked

Every input names its tick; the page records only inputs that change something (who leads, a way to walk, an act, a release, a choice,
a practice, vision, the end). The platform gives a run its seed and replays the record with the same rules on `run.submit`; every
milestone is decided by the state the run ends in. The verifier is the rules alone (`src/replay.ts`); its digest is pinned in
`configs/games/peaceful-savage.ts`, so a change to the rules is a change there.

## Milestones (40)

A hand on the tree, Savage Vision, Sparky, the Savage Breath; Kai, Zora, Mama Heerah and Dr. Ali found; a snake turns gold, six
threads, not fooled once; the jackets, the locked drawer, the card deck, Zora's garden, Kai's tower, pancakes, every lamp, curious hands,
every corner; six perfect keys; fifty motes and all the plasma; a relic and every relic, every clue; mirror force; a key mastered, six
keys mastered, the family huddle; level 3 and fully lit; Kai's hologram, Zora's masterpiece, Mama's song and Daddy's relic; and the tree
lit with each of the four leading. No two are earned by the same event.

Each milestone's drawing (what it becomes on chain, and shows on pages) is in `milestone-arts/`, one file named for each milestone's id
(`found-kai.svg` is Kai found, `kai-hologram.svg` is Kai's hologram), so the staff page's "Define many milestones" takes the whole folder
at once. A test keeps the names and the milestones matched, and every drawing passing the platform's own check unchanged. The drawings
are a draft for the owner, like the list.

## Fairness

The seed decides only where things are (which of two places a clue lies in, where each snake circles) and which side each true answer
is on; every run has the same story and the same things to find. A believed trick dims the spark and moves its snake on, and a breath
always brings the spark back. Only three tricks believed with the spark low greys the lead out of the run, and every true answer and
whole breath between brings a step of colour back. Beats and breaths have windows of give that grow with the lead's stats and mastery.

## Tests

`pnpm --filter @game/peaceful-savage test:unit`, `test:integration`, `test:coverage`. Bots play whole runs with each lead and prove
every milestone can be earned; weak play still finishes. The 3D view is tested without WebGL (a stand-in renderer) by showing it a whole
run, every kind of moment and every shot, the finale, the farewell and the grey ending; the farewell's choreography is tested too (the
children in front in the hug, the arms behind them, no hand before a face, Sparky below every face, the held hands meeting). How it looks
was checked in a browser. The page is played through the bundled game against a simulated platform.

## Dependency

`three@0.187.0` (and `@types/three@0.187.0` for the tests), pinned, the same version Pocket Dragon uses. Only `src/view/` imports it;
the verifier has none of it. Its MIT licence is shipped as `assets/third-party-notices.txt`.

## Drafts for the owner

The title, the summary, the hero cards, every line in `src/script/`, the stats, the milestone list and the cover are drafts. Canon is
the author's: nothing here names the Serpent or prints the tongue's decoder, and the seventh hand-key is never playable.
